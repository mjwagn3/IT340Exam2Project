1.	What caused low accuracy
Our model achieves only about 70% accuracy because it is learning from noise in the dataset. Noise consists of words that occur frequently but do not help distinguish between spam and ham emails. Examples include common words such as "the," "a," "with," "this," and many others. These words appear in both spam and ham emails, so they provide little useful information about the email's category. Because the model learns from these words, they can influence the classification process even though they have little predictive value. As a result, the model may place less emphasis on more informative words and incorrectly classify some emails.
2.	Why it can happen:
The reason this can happen is that we did not filter out common words before classifying the emails. Words such as "the," "a," and "with" appear frequently in both spam and ham emails. If we do not remove these words during preprocessing, the model will learn from them even though they provide little useful information. By filtering out these common words, we tell the model to ignore them and focus on more informative words that help distinguish between spam and ham emails. This reduces noise in the dataset and can improve classification accuracy.
